## Comparison

All three runs reached the same matter and the same answer, and all failed only the key-terms memo criterion. Each run opened the memo (T0 among four closing reads at [54], T1 in a 15-document screening batch at [42], T2 right after listing the chosen matter's folder at [86]-[88]), and none cited it. The answers list the execution-version agreement, side letter, subscription agreement and closing checklist (all criteria the judge passed), plus funds-flow and opinion papers, but not the memo. No review asked for the memo, and the write reviews checked dates, terms and recency.

The routes differed. T0 used a path search, a store-wide scan and its aggregation, screened four candidates and then read the chosen matter's closing papers. T1 aggregated its scan in agent-defined stages, read 15 documents of five candidates and one hit document from each of eight more, and after writing ran two agent-defined recency checks, labelled here as check stages. T2's first scans were filtered to committee-term lines and did not show the matter. It listed files of about 20 hand-picked matters across four listing attempts, and only its agent-defined unfiltered full-store scan surfaced the matter, after which it read the matter in depth.

Label checks: start notes were ignored three times (a range scan under an in-matter label in T0, folder counts under a store-listing label and first candidate reads under an in-depth label in T2). End notes: T0 and T1 followed the scan label after a path-search stage was judged scan-like; T1 kept the check label for further source re-reads after an in-depth note (ignored); the others came on stages with no later work of that kind or led to another label.
