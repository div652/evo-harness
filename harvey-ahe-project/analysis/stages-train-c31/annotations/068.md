## Comparison

All three runs fail only the precision criterion and pass the device compliance review with its proof documents. Every run listed the store, scanned text and paths for regulator and approval terms, listed and screened candidate matters, and wrote. They differ in how widely they read 'turned on': T0 read a small set in depth and added only one extra (the drug-promotion inquiry); T1 and T2 read about 30 documents across many candidates and listed eight matters, counting offerings, asset sales and litigations whose documents mention an approved product. Reviews did not explain the difference: T0's in-depth review called all three inclusions thin, T1's write review even called the reading possibly narrow, and only T2's write review asked to tighten a broad scope, which the agent ignored. Label notes were ignored where they applied.
