## Comparison

All three runs pass with the same procedure: a matter-folder listing (T0, T1), store-wide extraction scans narrowed from a loose pattern (outputs of about 363K characters in T2 and 1.23M in T1) to exact request phrases, one or two parallel screening batches of complaints and status documents across the hit matters, a dated filing confirmation for the lead matter from its status letter or motion memo, and an answer that cites the lead matter's complaint, lists older filed cases and excludes a later unfiled draft. T0 added agent-declared store-wide date scans and a per-matter complaint check before writing; T1 checked the runner-up's filing date in an agent-declared recency stage and, after writing, screened the five surfaced matters a check review named, adding one sentence to the answer; T2 ran one more filename-prefiltered date scan. All agent-declared stages map onto existing scan, within-matter search, in-depth reading and check stages.

Label checks: start notes were ignored twice (T0 compared saved scan sets under a within-matter search label; T1 counted folders under a store-listing label) and never followed. End-of-stage notes were followed three times (T2 declared a scan after its snippet stage; T1 declared screening and then a check stage as the notes named) and ignored twice (T1 put snippet and reading work under an agent-defined recency stage); three end notes had no later work of that kind.
