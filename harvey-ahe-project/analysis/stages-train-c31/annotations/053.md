## Comparison

All three runs fail, each on several criteria, and the errors differ in detail but share one stage. Every run built its candidate set from store-wide text scans (with timeouts and loose patterns flagged by the reviews) and read the same dozen offering matters. At the write stage, every run asserted matters outside the requested set as qualifying (a CLO note issue in all three, plus three planned or terminated deals in T1), and dropped one closed matter after reading its closing records (T0 and T1 the add-on placement, judged not shown closed; T2 the bank placement, judged pending). Earlier document-choice errors differ: T0 never opened the executed-but-terminated matter and skipped the listed global-note forms; T1 read other documents than the closing index its scan output showed; T2 read the terminated matter's preliminary documents instead of the executed purchase agreement shown in its listing. Reviews flagged hand-picked candidate sets in generic terms, named the add-on placement's closing status in T0 and T1 (not acted on), and never questioned whether the CLO deal belongs to the requested practice group. Label notes were mostly ignored; no agent-defined stages occurred.
