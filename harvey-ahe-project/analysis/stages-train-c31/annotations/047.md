Reviews flagged the file-name filter, the hand-picked reads and the weak inclusion; T1 acted on none of them except re-reading two candidates. Label notes were mostly ignored (T2 path search under a store-listing label and screening under an in-depth label); T2's two late new stages came from reviewer step suggestions.
