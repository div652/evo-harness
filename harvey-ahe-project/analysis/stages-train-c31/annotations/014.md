## Comparison

All three runs failed the same way, at the write stage: each found both qualifying facilities after store-wide extraction scans and reads of executed agreements, then wrote the same two further term loans, read in the Financing folders of other deal matters, as qualifying facilities (four in every answer). The routes differ only in where the extra facilities entered: T1 and T2 read both in their first screening batch; T0 read one there and added the other after a review-suggested store-wide sweep of executed agreements. T1 also paired borrowers and amounts with the other matters after a parallel read batch and fixed this in its check stages. No review questioned whether the extra facilities belonged to the credit book: the write and check reviews asked for fact and path checks and told the agent not to change the set, and in T0 the last review said to keep all four (review-endorsed; T1 and T2 not-flagged). The agent declared many new stages (scans and summaries of saved scan output, listings of surfaced matters, coverage checks, post-writing reconciliations); all map onto existing stages and none changed the set.

Label checks: start notes were ignored twice, both in T2 (a folder count under a store-listing label, screening reads under a within-matter search label) and never followed. End-of-stage notes were ignored five times (T0 twice, T1 twice, T2 once), each time because the next stage doing that work was declared as an agent-defined new stage or under another taxonomy label; three end notes in T2 had no later work of that kind.

## Task context

Request: how many of the firm's credit facilities are covenant-lite in the market sense (institutional term loans with no always-on financial maintenance covenant), and which ones, for a lender update. Rubric: 1005-00001 (Nexford TLB) and 1021-00001 (Verimark TLB) qualify, plus a precision criterion. 1001-00007 (Ardent) and 1041-00003 (Cloudium), term loans with revolver-only springing covenants documented in the Financing folders of other deal matters, are outside the permitted set.
