## Comparison

All three runs fail, every spread statistic failing with the set. Each run's store-wide extraction scans (and path searches) surfaced every qualifying settlement. T0 and T2 first went wrong at screening (surfaced-not-opened): T0 read about fifteen settlement agreements and never opened one large paid settlement; T2 read a hand-picked set of 15 matters and left four qualifying ones unopened, listing one as excluded unread. T1 read the agreements of all qualifying matters under an agent-declared classification stage and a reviewer-suggested widening stage, so its first error sits at the write stage (under-inclusion of two read settlements). All three answers also share later write-stage errors: settlements read but dropped, paying side reversed for one or two matters, and two or three matters outside the set counted. Screening reviews flagged unopened matters in generic terms each time; specific reviews in T1 and T2 (a read matter dropped without reason, a doubtful inclusion) were not acted on. Agent-declared stages in T1 and T2 were all existing stages (scans, path searches, screening, checks); in T2 four declared check steps after writing changed nothing.

Label checks: start-of-stage notes fired at most stage starts and end-of-stage label notes at five stage ends. The agent followed a note once (T0 closed an empty content-search stage and opened the suggested extraction-scan stage) and ignored it six times (folder counts under the store-listing label in T0 and T1; a store-wide scan under a screening label in T0; first reads under the listing-read label and in-depth reads under it in T2; in T1 the reads after a listing-read note went under a reviewer-suggested new stage instead).
