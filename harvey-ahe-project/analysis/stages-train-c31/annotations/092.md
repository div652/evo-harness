## Comparison

All fail, in different ways. All three runs built store-wide scan pools that contained every needed matter. T0 (3/9) and T1 (4/9) chose what to read by covenant-named files, which led mainly to sale-of-business agreements in deal matters, and each never opened at least one surfaced employment matter (first error at the stage that chose what to open; reviews there flagged unopened matters only in long generic lists). T0 then wrote a single matter; T1 wrote fifteen, counting deal covenants as employment-scoped despite a write review naming that broad reading. T2 (7/9) screened by engagement letters and complaints, reached three of the four matters and passed precision, but excluded the fourth after only listing its files (templates folder), with no review naming it. Agent-declared new stages (scan aggregation, recall validation, reconciliation, audits) mapped to existing scan, search and check stages; label notes were ignored where they applied.
