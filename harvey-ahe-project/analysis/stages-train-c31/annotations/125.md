## Comparison

All three runs fail, the same way in kind. Each scanned the whole store into a pool of 150-220 hit matters and then read only a hand-picked 12-25 pleadings, so one to four needed matters were never opened (T0 one, T1 two, T2 four); each excluded the restrictive-covenant matter as employment after reading its complaint; and each excluded the bank matter as defence-side. The bank exclusion differs: T0 and T1 wrote it so after a review named the bank as plaintiff, while T2 first included it and then removed it in a post-write reconciliation stage that followed a review's advice to count only matters where the client pleads damages. T1 and T2 also asserted arbitration matters outside the set. Reviews flagged unopened matters in every stage but named the missed matters only in T0. Agent-declared new stages (a classification step and scans in T0, reconciliation checks in T2) all mapped to existing stages; T2's reconciliation is where one error entered.
