## Comparison

All three runs failed, and all three in the same first way: each had a kept vehicle's carry agreement in its own output (T0 in a candidate listing and scan snippets, T1 in its scan hits and filtered listing, T2 in its name screen) but chose that vehicle's partnership-agreement amendment, memos and side letters to read, and later name or same-document filters dropped the file, so the proof document was never cited (first error at the first reading stage after it appeared). T0 and T1 otherwise wrote all four vehicles with their documents (10 of 11). T2 also excluded two vehicles it had read, one as still in formation and one as not showing the clawback (5 of 11). The reviews differed: T2's listing review named carry-type files of that matter as at risk and was ignored; in T0 and T1 the reviews pushed the other way (calling the carry snippet irrelevant, or the carry files entity papers, and asking for partnership agreements and side letters), and the agents followed. Agent-declared stages (six in T0, one in T1, two in T2) were scans, listings, verification reads and an audit that map onto existing stages; none played a role in the shared error.

Label checks: start notes were ignored eight times (T0 3, T1 1, T2 4: folder counting under the store-listing label, a path search under the document-type label, scans and screening reads under reading or in-matter labels) and never followed. End notes were followed once (T0 declared the folder-listing stage next) and ignored twice (T0 and T2 declared their next reading stages as their own new stages rather than the listing-read stage); the other end notes had no later work of that kind.
