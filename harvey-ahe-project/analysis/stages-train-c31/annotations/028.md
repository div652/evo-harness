## Comparison

Mixed (T1 passes; T0 and T2 fail). All three runs found and read the same five matters through path searches and store-wide text scans, then screened dozens of other scan hits from cover pages. They split at the write and check stages, over two borderline matters. T0 wrote a stalking-horse buyer's matter as an additional adjacent matter; the write review questioned it, and a later check re-read its engagement letter but kept it (over-inclusion). T1 wrote the same adjacent matter but removed it in its second check pass. Then, when it moved the standby-package matter out of the count, the review warned that this removed a qualifying matter, and T1 kept that matter as a separate finding. In T2 the reviews pushed the other way: the review at the second check called the standby matter a weak-grounds inclusion, so the agent moved it out; it restored the matter after re-reading; the next review called that a reinstatement and asked to restore the four-matter headline, which the agent did (under-inclusion, review-endorsed). The critic's reviews differed at the split, and in T2 they caused the failure. Label notes and agent-defined stages (extra screening and check stages) played no part in the split.
