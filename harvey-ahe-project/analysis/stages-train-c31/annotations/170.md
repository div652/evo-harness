## Comparison

All fail; the runs found the same core matters (the outside-date lapse, the regulatory-filing withdrawal and, in T1 and T2, the terminated deal and a merger ended on regulatory grounds) and missed the same group of four. They failed at different stages. T0's first error is its narrower phrase rescan, which never returned four needed matters (no review named them), after which a decisive-file-name filter dropped two more (reviewed and partly acted on). T1 abandoned its only content scan with five of six ranges unfinished despite a specific review flag. T2 completed its scan, but chose a hand-picked set to read and never opened three matters its own path search and no-closing regex showed; harness unopened-matter lists named one of them from early on. T0 and T2 each added a matter outside the set (a dormant deal; a merger still in litigation, flagged by T2's write review). Label notes were followed once (T2 declared a scan after a path search) and otherwise had no effect.
