## Comparison

All three runs fail, the same way in kind but with different reach. Their store-wide scans surfaced all nine needed matters, and each run then chose what to read from a hand-picked or filtered subset: T0 dropped one matter through a both-term count filter and skipped a listed candidate; T1 read a 15-matter selection and left out three; T2 skipped one in its first screening and in every later list. All three also asserted matters outside the set (executed or draft-stage deals), and T0/T1 cited drafts, letters or redlines instead of the proof documents. The reviews repeatedly flagged unopened matters and draft proof documents, and T1's named one omitted matter twice; the agents answered by re-scanning or re-listing rather than opening the named matters. T2 recovered two matters T1 missed by reading new candidates late. Label notes and new stages played no role.
