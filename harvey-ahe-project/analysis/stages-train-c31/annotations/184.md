Each then picked a handful of candidates by recency guesses or hand-made shortlists and never opened most of those matters (surfaced-not-opened): at T0's first reading stage, at T1's listing of the eight highest-numbered matters, and at T2's screening reads. Every run also opened a joint-venture matter that qualifies and wrote it off as closed. T0 and T2 asserted a deal outside the set. Reviews flagged hand-picked shortlists in every run, named the guessed number range in T1, and said little about closing status. The agent-defined stages (T0's batched extraction, triage and per-matter reads) and the label notes did not widen the candidate set.
