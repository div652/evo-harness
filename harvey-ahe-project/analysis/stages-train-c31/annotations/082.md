## Comparison

All three runs listed the store, counted matter folders, and searched paths and document text for device, IP and injunction words. Store-wide extraction scans timed out repeatedly in every run, and each run tried a different workaround in agent-defined stages: T0 ran chunked series scans, T1 a tightened co-occurrence scan and a four-batch rescan, and T2 a background scan it polled. All three then read the leading patent matter's injunction papers: a preliminary-injunction motion marked as a contingency draft on hold, a permanent-injunction analysis, and a proposed order. They also read a few injunction matters outside the device field.

T0 and T1 wrote a no-qualifying-matter answer directly; both write reviews warned of a possibly narrow reading, and the agents kept the conclusion. T2 first wrote the leading matter as a strong match. Its write review pointed out that the papers were a proposed order and an on-hold draft, and the next check stage rewrote the bottom line to a strict no-verified-injunction answer. Three more check passes refined the wording, and the stage-count note fired twice. In this run the reviewer's correction turned an answer that would have failed into a passing one.

Label checks: T0 and T1 counted folders under a store-listing label (start note ignored), and T0 read three candidates under an in-depth label that the start note pointed to screening (ignored). End notes (document-type search, listing reads) came on stages with no later work of that kind. T2 had no label notes.
