## Comparison

The runs share the arc: whole-store glob and folder inventory, a store-wide extraction scan for practice-group and consent terms (timed out and split in T1 and T2), then per-matter verification over a candidate union of about 30 matters. T0 and T1 declared their own verification stages (screening reads, scripts over saved hits, a working audit table in T0); T2 kept taxonomy labels throughout.

The outcome hinges on how loosely a matter is counted. T0 and T1 kept two or three borderline matters after reviews named them; their re-checks found literal passages and treated these as enough. T2's exact-term scan stage summarised the right three matters, but the reviewer called the exclusions unbacked by reading, and the agent's snippet review then counted thirteen more matters on single documents. The critic thus flagged the over-inclusion in T0 and T1 (not acted on) and pushed toward it in T2.
