## Comparison

All three runs listed the store (T1 and T2 printed every file path, 650-700K characters), searched file names for covenant words and ran store-wide extraction scans. The executed nine-month settlement covenant surfaced in every run.

T0 (pass) followed the reviewer's suggested steps closely: a compact per-matter duration rescan, then an agent-defined per-matter verification that listed 16 matters and read six candidates' agreements, including the execution-version settlement. It then read that settlement's exhibit, consent order and signature pages before writing the nine-month term as the answer, with the 12-month separation agreement and a six-month term by other counsel as comparators. Six check stages followed, two of them refused calls and two with stage-count notes.

T1 (fail) first read 13 non-compete agreements picked from its file-name hits and settled on an 18-month term. A store-wide duration scan then led to a 12-month separation agreement, and an agent-defined duration check (reviewer-suggested) led to the nine-month settlement. Its answer gave the nine-month term only if a settlement counts and named the 12-month agreement as the shortest standalone term. The judge counted that as a second matter asserted.

T2 (fail) saved a file-name list that included the settlement matter but read ten agreements from eight other matters, then went deep on the 12-month separation agreement and answered with it. The reviews asked about unopened matters only in general terms; the appended unopened-matters list named the settlement matter at three stage ends.

So T0 and T1 split at the write stage (wording of the answer), and T2 split off at screening.

Label checks: start notes were ignored four times (folder counts under a store-listing label in T0, a request-keyword path search under a document-type label and a scan under a screening label in T1, and two scans under path-search and in-matter labels in T2). End notes: T2 followed the scan label in its next scan; T0 kept the check label for later source checks, and T1 kept the screening label for its next reads (both ignored); the other end notes had no later work of that kind or led to a different label.
