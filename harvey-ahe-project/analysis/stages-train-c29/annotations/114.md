## Comparison

All three runs failed, each by both omitting needed matters and asserting many others (precision failed in all three). They failed at different stages. T0 ran a store-wide file-name search for expert-retention files that likely reached most needed matters but displayed only the matters its timed-out text scan had already named, so five never entered the candidate set (over-narrow filter, flagged by the critic and ignored). T1's tight text scan surfaced all ten, but its screening reads skipped two of them and its answer excluded three it had opened, judging from one expert letter each (surfaced, not opened). T2 got closest (12/17): an extraction script over expert-named files reached nine of ten, but the one matter without expert-named files needed a full-text scan, which the agent stopped while it was still running after the critic had flagged that (search miss); it also excluded one opened matter as pre-filing. In every run the reviews repeated generic 'many surfaced matters unopened' warnings, and the over-inclusion flags led at most to downgrading one or two matters.
