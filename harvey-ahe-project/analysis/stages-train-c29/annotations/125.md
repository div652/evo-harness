## Comparison

All three runs failed, each in a different way after the same opening (capped listing, folder listing, a store-wide scan that timed out). T0's fallback file-name search hit its own line cap and its later scans filtered on one damages word, so three matters never surfaced (search miss at the file-name search; the critic flagged the cap and the agent's fix stayed too narrow). T1 and T2 surfaced every qualifying matter with file-name searches but chose subsets to open: T1 left three unread at its single big screening stage (critic flagged unopened candidates generically, ignored), T2 left one out at its candidate listing (critic flagged, the reopened stage did not reach it). All three runs then excluded matters they had read on narrow category judgements and asserted the same non-qualifying arbitration matter; reviews flagged some exclusions late and the agents finished without acting.
