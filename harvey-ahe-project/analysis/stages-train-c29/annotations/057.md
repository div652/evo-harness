## Comparison

All three runs failed the same way at the writing stage. Each surfaced all four needed matters through store-wide scans and file-name searches, opened the two clear formation matters, and then classed the other two needed matters (an amendment and a key-person event matter) as post-formation work and excluded them, while counting a master-vehicle reorganisation as a formation. They differed only in how much of the excluded matters they saw: T0 listed their files but read nothing, T1 read an amendment and an event memo, T2 saw scan snippets. The critic never named the two excluded matters: every write-stage review gave only a generic under-inclusion flag (T0's also said exclusions may rest on snippets), and in T2 an earlier review endorsed one exclusion. The extra matter was flagged specifically only in T1, and kept.
