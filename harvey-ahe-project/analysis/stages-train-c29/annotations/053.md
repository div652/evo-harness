## Comparison

All three runs failed. They shared the procedure: store listing, folder listing, store-wide extraction scans (with timeouts and reruns), then batches of opening reads across the shortlisted matters, one write and a path check. All three counted a structured-credit matter as qualifying at the write stage (over-inclusion). T0 differed in excluding an executed-then-terminated deal it had read (first error at the write stage). T1 and T2 failed first at their first screening reads, which skipped a closing index they had listed, and then bucketed one or two closed deals as not closed and added unclosed matters. Reviews at every stage repeated the same generic flags (unopened surfaced matters, possible under-inclusion); the write review's over-inclusion flag in T1 led to two revision passes that left the extra matters in place.
