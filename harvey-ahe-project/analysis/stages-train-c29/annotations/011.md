## Comparison

All three runs failed, partly in the same way. All surfaced every needed matter through store-wide scans; all failed the proof-document criterion for the matter whose required document is a closing memo, which T0 even read, and for one or two more, because they cited executed agreements, negotiation memos or redlines chosen by snippet scripts rather than opening the required documents (listed-document-not-opened, first error in T0 and T2); and all listed extra matters on drafts or snippets (over-inclusion). T1 failed earlier and worse: it viewed its range scans only through line caps and a threshold filter that dropped needed matters (first error, flagged generically and only partly fixed), then left one surfaced matter unopened and dropped one it had read. The critic flagged proof documents and over-inclusion only generically; specific flags (T0 over-inclusion, T1 surfaced and dropped matters) were ignored.
