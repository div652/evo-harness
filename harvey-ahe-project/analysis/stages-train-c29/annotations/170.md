## Comparison

All three runs failed the same way: the content scans timed out or were narrowed, candidates were chosen through file-name words (termination, withdrawal, status), and qualifying deals whose files carry no such words were never opened. T0 and T1 never surfaced one deal at all (search miss) and left out others their searches showed (surfaced, never opened); T2's broad name search showed all eight but its hand-picked scan set left out three. T0 and T2 also kept stalled or dormant deals as non-closings (over-inclusion) after reviews named them; T1 passed the precision criterion. Reviews flagged unopened matters generically in every run.
