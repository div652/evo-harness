## Comparison

All three failed, with the same mix of errors and no single split. Every run surfaced all nine qualifying matters through store-wide scans; then each chose what to read from a hand-picked shortlist, so in T1 and T2 qualifying matters were never opened (surfaced-not-opened, first error), and in all three the proof documents for two or three matters were substitutes (redlines, comments letters, term sheets) although the needed documents were shown in their own outputs (listed-document-not-opened; T0's first error). All three also listed extra draft or commitment-stage matters (over-inclusion). In T0 the critic drove a late narrowing: the write review asked to drop draft matters, the edit dropped a qualifying matter, the next review named it, and the one after pushed back to the narrow headline (review-endorsed). Reviews mostly gave generic hand-pick warnings; specific flags named drafts (T1, T2) and one substitute proof (T1), and the agent only relabelled.
