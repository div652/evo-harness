## Comparison

All three runs failed, all finding every needed add-back deal through store-wide file-name searches and chunked scans, then reading executed agreements. They split on how the population was fixed. T0 and T2 counted too many deals as signed deals with add-backs (over-inclusion at the write stage; T2 also put two sponsor deals in the corporate bucket after mispaired parallel reads). T1 fixed a hand-picked list after its scans and left out a corporate deal that its own file-name search had shown (incomplete population at the last scan stage). In every run a review named the matter or list at fault (the court-approved facility in T0 and T2, the hand-picked list in T1) and the agent kept its set; label notes were ignored twice in T0 and once in T2.
