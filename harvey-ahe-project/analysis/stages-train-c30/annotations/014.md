## Comparison

All three runs failed, two of them the same way. T0 and T2 found both qualifying facilities through file-name searches, partial extraction scans and reads of executed agreements, then wrote term loans read in the Financing folders of other deal matters as covenant-lite facilities too (three in T0, two in T2). They differ only in how wide the extra set was. In both, every review after the write stage asked for fact and path checks and told the agent to leave the population unchanged unless a source contradicted it; no review questioned whether those facilities belonged to the credit book, so the error is not-flagged. T1 went through the same search sequence but printed multi-hundred-thousand-character scan outputs (flagged by the scan review at [43]); in the screening stage, which it kept under the declared label "Read documents picked from the file listing" despite the stage_start note, a greedy snippet script over seven named agreements returned about 810K characters and the run ended with no deliverable. The stage_start label notes mostly did not apply (the declared label fitted the work); the one that did (T1 screening) was ignored.

## Task context

Request: how many of the firm's credit facilities are covenant-lite in the market sense (institutional term loans with no always-on financial maintenance covenant), and which ones, for a lender update. Rubric: 1005-00001 (Nexford TLB) and 1021-00001 (Verimark TLB) qualify, plus a precision criterion. 1001-00007, 1003-00003 and 1041-00003 (term loans with springing or no covenants documented in the Financing folders of other deal matters) are outside the permitted set.
