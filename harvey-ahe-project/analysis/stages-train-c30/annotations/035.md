Mixed (T2 passes; T0, T1 fail). All three runs reached the same candidates through path searches and text-extraction scans, screened them, and first wrote a five-matter answer that counted withdrawn and draft offerings. They split at the check stages, where the critic's reviews differed. In T2 the check review recommended leading with the two completed offerings and labelling the other three as reference-only; the agent did so and passed. In T0 the check review flagged that the draft matters were still counted, and the agent finished without changing the list; T0 had also never opened one qualifying matter's lock-up form, which its screening review had flagged. In T1 the write review suggested that a later secondary-offering matter's earlier offering might qualify; the agent added it, removed it after the next review called it a different deal, and re-added it after a further review called the removal narrowing.
