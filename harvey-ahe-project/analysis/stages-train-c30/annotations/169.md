## Comparison

All three runs failed (12, 15 and 15 of 19). The search phase differed (T0 fought timed-out scans and guessed paths; T1 merged file-name and content-scan unions; T2 used a per-matter aggregate scan), but every run reached and opened every needed matter: the executed facility agreements or engagement letters of all nine were read or listed before writing. The runs failed on the same judgement at the write stage: the request's carve-out for real-estate-secured mortgage/construction financings was read as covering any corporate facility whose collateral package included mortgages or real property, or whose proceeds funded construction. T0 and T2 excluded three such facilities, T1 two (it kept the third, an aerospace revolver whose deal memo it had read). The proof-document criteria for the excluded matters failed with them. T0 also listed a revolver that the other runs had found to be governed by another state's law, and T2 listed an acquisition financing that T1 had excluded as M&A-side work.

Critic: no review questioned the exclusions on their merits. In T1 the screening review named the two matters but only asked to check their collateral against the carve-out, which is the basis the agent had already used. In T0 and T2 the write-stage reviews gave stock advice to re-check exclusions against the carve-outs; the check stages verified cited paths and amounts and left the scope unchanged, and later reviews told the agent not to add or drop matters without clear evidence.

Label notes: in all three runs the agent screened candidates under "Read documents picked from the file listing" or "Read a chosen matter's documents in depth" after being shown the Screen suggestion (ignored), and T0 listed folder names and counts under the store-listing label twice (ignored).
