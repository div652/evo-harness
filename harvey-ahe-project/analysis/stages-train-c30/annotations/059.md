## Comparison

All three runs failed, with one shared error and two run-specific ones. Shared: every run wrote matters known to it only from draft side letters, a first-draft agreement, a tax-response narrative or a lapsed negotiation as qualifying precedents, usually with status labels, instead of leaving them out. The reviews flagged this before or at the write stage in all three runs (T0: a named third-draft side letter at [54] and "drafts only in exclusions" at [88]; T2: a named undated side letter at [81]; T1: a generic draft warning at [90]), and later reviews told the agent to keep the matter set unchanged, so the agent never removed them. T0 is the only run that found all four qualifying matters: after its first screening it ran a second, prefiltered scan that surfaced the co-invest matter with its executed agreement and read it. T1 and T2 split from T0 at the same point but differently: T1's scan surfaced the other co-invest matter (and the review at [38] warned that later matters in the capped output might be unreviewed), yet its screening left it out (surfaced-not-opened); T2 listed that matter's files at [43]-[45] and then never read them (under-inclusion, not flagged). T1 also cited only a side letter for a co-invest matter whose executed partnership agreement never appeared in its capped scan views. Where the stage_start label notes applied (screening declared as "Read documents picked from the file listing" in T0, scan summary and screening declared as "Search within a candidate matter's files" in T2), the agent kept the declared label.
