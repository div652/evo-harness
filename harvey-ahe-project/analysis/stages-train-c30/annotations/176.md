Reviews were cautious throughout (mostly "partly"), asking for name variants and for surfaced matters to be opened; following them cost extra stages but did not change the answer. Label notes: T0 did the folder-name-and-count listing under the store-listing label (ignored), T1 screened the ten matters under "Read documents picked from the file listing" (ignored); T2 had no applicable note.
