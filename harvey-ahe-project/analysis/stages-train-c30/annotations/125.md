## Comparison

All three runs fail, with 22, 16 and 21 of 29 criteria. All built a candidate set from path searches and extraction scans, read one or two pleadings per chosen matter, and wrote a long list with exclusions. They share three judgement errors at the write stage: a commercial claim by a former-employer company excluded as employment litigation (T1, T2; T0 never opened it), a shipping matter excluded as maritime specialty (T0, T2; T1 confused its identity through parallel-read pairing), and a bank's matter excluded as defended-only after reading only its answer to counterclaims; all three also count an arbitration counterclaim matter outside the set. First errors differ: T0 and T1 left surfaced matters unopened at the screening stage (one in T0, three in T1), T2 read the original petition but not the listed damages motion of one matter. Reviews flagged unopened surfaced matters only generically and named the defended-only exclusion once (T1), which the agent ignored.
