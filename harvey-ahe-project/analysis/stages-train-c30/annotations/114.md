## Comparison

All three runs fail in the same way. Each built a candidate list from file-name searches for expert and retention words and text scans, then read retention letters picked from that list: the first error in all three is at that screening stage, where surfaced matters whose expert appears only in a mediation statement, a case memo or an internal retention memo were left unopened (one in T0, four in T1, two in T2). The answers then count forensic, technical and arbitration engagements as damages experts, and T0 and T1 read the internal retention memo of one needed matter but excluded it as a recommendation without an executed letter. Screening reviews in all runs warned generically about unopened matters; T0's reviews named the excluded memo matter three times without effect, and the write reviews of T0 and T2 named the broad scope, which the check stages left unchanged.
