All three runs fail in the same way. Each reached every needed matter through broad path searches and text-extraction scans, then screened only part of them: the first error in all three is at the screening stage, where surfaced matters were left unopened (two firm-commitment matters and the direct placement in T0; several note offerings and the direct placement in T1; four firm-commitment matters and the direct placement in T2).
